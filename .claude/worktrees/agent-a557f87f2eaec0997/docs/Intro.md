This portal, betacareers.muonline.ac.in is a redesign attempt of careers.amuonline.ac.in 
The objective is to improve the portal in every aspect and that's why we have gathered a lot of documents from various domains, the regulations and rules from the UGC, and the FN1 and FN3 older forms, which used to be offline when people used to fill them up and print and submit them. This also would give you the idea. This is just a starting point that I am giving you. You have to do your own research. I first analyse all the documents present in the docs folder and create a comprehensive understanding. If there is anything missing, go ahead and search on the internet to find more relevant and authentic content in this regard. I will also provide some images in the documentation folder for the design difference of how we are expecting the designs to look. Apart from that the intendant portal, we would expect to be very secure in terms of security and a very functional one as well. 

This portal will have two phases:
1. The front end, which is for the prospective employees who will sign up on the front end of this website and fill all the relevant modules and submit the application form by making the payment.
2. The admin side, which will be used by the administrators of this application to create various advertisements, post, side, and configure various aspects of the fees and everything. They would also be able to download all the reports and see the progress of the advertisements in the form of charts. The admin panel would also have a mechanism to update the scrutinies, generate assigned roll numbers for the applicants for a particular post or for a particular advertisement, download their entrance admit card, download their interview call letters, send them invitation data, and everything.
There will be a mass emailing service where the administrators can select the post of a particular post or of a particular advertisement and send bulk emails for information to the users.

The documents provided in the docs folder are not final in any way. These are just the starting point for your research.
1. You have to analyse these local documents.
2. If you find your thing and you believe that there is any gap, then to complete your research go ahead on the internet and search for that information but try to be using the authentic sources such as the Ministry of Education or the UGC's official portal.
