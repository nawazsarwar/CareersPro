## 1. Executive Overview & Legal Mandate

The University Grants Commission (UGC) lays down statutory benchmarks to govern the direct recruitment and Career Advancement Scheme (CAS) promotions for academic faculty across all Central, State, Deemed, and Private Universities as well as constituent/affiliated colleges in India.

The governing regulation is the **UGC Regulations 2018**, published in the Gazette of India on July 18, 2018, as amended by the **UGC (2nd Amendment) Regulations 2023** (effective July 1, 2023).

## 2. Cadre-Wise Minimum Eligibility Qualifications

### Assistant Professor (Academic Level 10 / Entry Pay Rs. 57,700/-)

Direct recruitment for disciplines of Arts, Commerce, Humanities, Education, Law, Social Sciences, Sciences, Languages, Library Science, Physical Education, and Journalism & Mass Communication follows two pathways (**Option A** or **Option B**):

#### Option A:
1. **Master’s Degree**: A Master’s degree with **55% marks** (or an equivalent grade in a point-scale wherever the grading system is followed) in a concerned/relevant/allied subject from an Indian University, or an equivalent degree from an accredited foreign university.
2. **National Eligibility Test (NET)**: Must have cleared the **National Eligibility Test (NET)** conducted by the UGC or the CSIR, or a similar test accredited by the UGC, like **SLET/SET**.
3. **Ph.D. Exemption Clause**: Candidates who have been awarded a Ph.D. Degree in accordance with the University Grants Commission (Minimum Standards and Procedure for Award of M.Phil./Ph.D. Degree) Regulations, 2009 or 2016 and their amendments from time to time, are exempted from NET/SLET/SET.
   * *Conditions for exemption*: The candidate must produce a certificate from the competent authority (Registrar/Dean) verifying:
     - Ph.D. degree awarded in regular mode only;
     - Ph.D. thesis evaluated by at least two external examiners;
     - Open Ph.D. viva voce conducted;
     - Candidate published at least two research papers from Ph.D. work, with at least one in a refereed journal;
     - Candidate presented at least two papers in conferences/seminars sponsored/funded/supported by UGC/ICSSR/CSIR or similar agency.

#### Option B:
* The Ph.D. degree has been obtained from a foreign university/institution with a ranking among the **top 500** in the World University Ranking (at any time) by any one of the following:
  1. Quacquarelli Symonds (QS)
  2. Times Higher Education (THE)
  3. Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) of the Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

---

### Associate Professor (Academic Level 13A / Entry Pay Rs. 1,31,400/-)

1. **Good academic record** with a **Ph.D. Degree** in the concerned/allied/relevant disciplines.
2. A **Master’s Degree with at least 55% marks** (or an equivalent grade on a point scale).
3. A minimum of **eight (8) years of experience** of teaching and/or research in an academic/research position equivalent to that of Assistant Professor in a University, College, or Accredited Research Institution/industry.
4. A minimum of **seven (7) publications** in peer-reviewed or UGC-CARE listed journals.
5. A total **Research Score of at least Seventy-Five (75)** as per the criteria given in Appendix II, Table 2 of UGC Regulations 2018.

---

### Professor (Academic Level 14 / Entry Pay Rs. 1,44,200/-)

#### Option A:
1. An eminent scholar having a **Ph.D. degree** in the concerned/allied/relevant discipline.
2. Published work of high quality, actively engaged in research with evidence of published work with a minimum of **ten (10) research publications** in peer-reviewed or UGC-CARE listed journals.
3. A total **Research Score of at least One Hundred and Twenty (120)** as per the criteria given in Appendix II, Table 2 of UGC Regulations 2018.
4. A minimum of **ten (10) years of teaching experience** in university/college as Assistant Professor/Associate Professor/Professor, and/or research experience at an equivalent level at the University/National Level Institutions with evidence of having successfully guided doctoral candidates.

#### Option B:
* An outstanding professional, having a Ph.D. degree in the relevant/allied/applied disciplines, from any academic institutions (not included in A) / industry, who has made significant contributions to the knowledge in the concerned/allied/relevant discipline, supported by documentary evidence, provided he/she has **ten (10) years’ experience**.

---

### Senior Professor (Academic Level 15 / Entry Pay Rs. 1,82,200/-)
* Up to 10% of the existing sanctioned strength of Professors in the university.
* Minimum of **ten (10) years of service as a Professor** in Academic Level 14.
* Selection is through an evaluation of curriculum vitae and ten best publications by a committee comprising three eminent international/national experts.

## 3. Conditions for Application & General Eligibility

### Relaxation Rules
* **5% Mark Relaxation (from 55% to 50%)**:
  - Provided at the Bachelor’s and Master’s level for candidates belonging to **SC / ST / OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) / Differently-abled (PwBD)** categories (Blindness & low vision, Deaf & hard of hearing, Locomotor disability including cerebral palsy, leprosy cured, dwarfism, acid attack victims and muscular dystrophy, Autism, intellectual disability, specific learning disability & mental illness).
* **Pre-1991 Master’s Degree Holders**:
  - A relaxation of 5% is provided (from 55% to 50%) to the Ph.D. Degree holders who obtained their Master’s Degree prior to **19 September 1991**.

### Ph.D. Degree Standards Compliance
* Candidates claiming exemption from NET based on Ph.D. awarded prior to July 11, 2009 must submit a certificate confirming compliance with UGC 2009/2016 minimum standards.

### UGC 2023 Amendment Regarding Ph.D. Requirement
* Under UGC Notification No. F. 9-1/2010(PS/PC)Pt. dated 30th June 2023 (*effective July 1, 2023*):
  > **"NET/SET/SLET shall be the minimum criteria for the direct recruitment to the post of Assistant Professor for all Higher Education Institutions."**
* The mandatory Ph.D. requirement for direct recruitment to Assistant Professor in Universities (which was previously deferred due to COVID-19) has been permanently relaxed to **optional**. However, Ph.D. holders continue to earn **30 points** in the shortlisting score matrix (Table 3A).

## 4. Shortlisting & Screening Criteria (Table 3A & Table 3B)

The UGC Regulations 2018 prescribe a 100-point objective scoring metric to filter and shortlist applicants for interview.

1. **M.Phil + Ph.D.**: Maximum capped at **30 points**.
2. **JRF / NET / SET**: Maximum capped at **07 points**.
3. **In Awards Category**: Maximum capped at **03 points**.

$$\text{Total Academic Score} = \sum (\text{Graduation} + \text{PG} + \text{M.Phil/Ph.D.} + \text{NET/JRF} + \text{Publications} + \text{Experience} + \text{Awards}) \le 100$$

### Screening Methodology & Cutoff Principles

1. **Shortlisting Solely for Interview Calling**:
   - The scores calculated under Table 3A / Table 3B are **only used for shortlisting candidates** for the interview.
   - **Crucial Rule**: These screening scores are **NOT** added to the interview score for deciding the final merit list.
2. **Number of Candidates Called**:
   - Universities typically determine a shortlisting ratio (e.g., top 15-20 candidates for 1 vacancy, and +5 for every additional vacancy in the same category) based on Table 3A rank.
   - Candidates tied at the cutoff score are all called for interview.

### Impact Factor & Joint Authorship Augmentation Rules

For Category 1 (Research Papers), paper scores are augmented based on Thomson Reuters / Clarivate **Impact Factor (IF)**:

1. **No Impact Factor**: Base score + **5 points**
2. **Impact Factor between 1 and 2**: Base score + **10 points**
3. **Impact Factor between 2 and 5**: Base score + **15 points**
4. **Impact Factor between 5 and 10**: Base score + **20 points**
5. **Impact Factor > 10**: Base score + **25 points**

#### Joint Authorship Sharing Formula:
* **Two authors**: 70% of total points to each author (i.e. $1.4 \times$ score split equally).
* **More than two authors**: 
  - **First / Principal / Corresponding Author**: **70% of total points**.
  - **Each Co-author**: **30% of total points**.

#### Category-Wise Score Caps for Associate Professor / Professor:
* Research Score must come from a minimum of **three (3) categories** out of the six categories.
* Score from Category 6 (Invited Lectures / Presentations) is capped at **maximum 30%** of the candidate's total research score.

## 6. Selection Committee Constitution & Interview Process

### Statutory Composition for Universities

1. **Chairperson**: Vice-Chancellor or their nominee.
2. **Visitor’s / Chancellor’s Nominee**: An academician nominated by the Visitor (in Central Universities) or Chancellor.
3. **Subject Experts**: **Three (3) external subject experts** not in service of the University, nominated by the Vice-Chancellor out of the approved panel.
4. **Dean of the Faculty**: Dean of the concerned Faculty.
5. **Head of the Department / Centre**: Head of Department (if post is at equivalent or lower rank).
6. **Affirmative Action Representative**: An academician representing SC / ST / OBC / Minority / Women / Differently-abled, nominated by the Vice-Chancellor if any candidate belongs to these categories.
7. **Quorum**: Minimum **four (4) members**, including at least **two (2) outside subject experts**.

## 7. Implementation Guidelines for the Recruitment Portal

To align the AMU Beta Careers portal with UGC compliance:

1. **Automated Table 3A Score Engine**:
   - Compute real-time graduation, PG, NET/JRF, M.Phil, Ph.D., and experience points dynamically with statutory capping rules automatically enforced.
2. **Dynamic Research Score Form (Table 2)**:
   - Provide structured input for ISSN, UGC-CARE list status, Impact Factor, authorship role (Principal vs. Co-author), calculating net weighted score automatically.
3. **Document Proof Enforcement**:
   - For every score-bearing claim (e.g. awards, NET certificates, Ph.D. compliance certificates, experience letters), mandate mandatory PDF attachment verification.
4. **Transparent Screening Workflow**:
   - Enable applicant dashboard to view itemized screening breakdown with automated grievance submission window before final interview shortlist generation.
